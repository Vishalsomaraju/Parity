RESIDENTIAL APARTMENT LEASE (HIGH-RESTRICTION)

This Lease Agreement is made on August 15, 2025, between Metro Urban Realty LLC ("Landlord") and Alex Morgan ("Tenant") for Unit 12C, Metro Towers.

1. RENT AND PENALTIES
Rent is $2,650.00 per month due promptly on the first (1st) day of each month. A late charge of $100.00 plus $20.00 per calendar day begins on the second (2nd) day of the month. Landlord reserves the right to refuse personal checks.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND NON-REFUNDABLE FEES
Tenant shall deposit $5,300.00 (two months' rent). Landlord shall retain a mandatory non-refundable $750.00 administrative and refurbishing fee upon departure. Remaining deposit funds may be held for ninety (90) days following lease expiration for final auditing.

3. LANDLORD ENTRY AND SURVEILLANCE
Landlord and building managers reserve the unrestricted right to enter the unit at any hour without prior notice for inspections, property viewings, or administrative checks. Tenant agrees that smart sensors installed by Landlord do not violate tenant privacy.

4. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE
Tenant accepts the apartment strictly in "AS-IS" condition. Tenant assumes financial responsibility for all maintenance, plumbing blockages, electrical fixture repairs, and appliance replacements exceeding $50.00 during the lease term.

5. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL AND TERMINATION
This lease automatically renews for an additional twelve (12) month term at a 15% rate increase unless Tenant delivers certified written notice at least one hundred twenty (120) days prior to lease expiration. Landlord may terminate this lease at any time with seven (7) days' notice.

6. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY
Tenant waives all claims against Landlord for damage, theft, water leaks, or personal injury occurring on the premises, and agrees to indemnify Landlord for all legal costs incurred in any landlord-tenant action.
