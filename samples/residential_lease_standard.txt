RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (STANDARD)

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made on August 15, 2025, between Oakwood Properties ("Landlord") and Alex Morgan ("Tenant") for the property located at 442 Elm Street, Unit 3B ("Premises").

1. TERM AND RENT
The term begins on September 1, 2025, and ends on August 31, 2026. Monthly rent is $2,200.00 payable on the first (1st) day of each month. A five (5) business day grace period applies before a 5% late fee is charged.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall deposit $2,200.00 as security. Landlord shall hold the deposit in a separate interest-bearing escrow account and return it within thirty (30) days of move-out, less documented deductions for actual damage beyond normal wear and tear.

3. LANDLORD RIGHT OF ENTRY
Landlord may enter the premises for inspections, repairs, or prospective tenant showings only upon giving at least twenty-four (24) hours' advance written notice, during reasonable daytime hours (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM), except in genuine emergencies.

4. MAINTENANCE AND HABITABILITY
Landlord warrants that the Premises are habitable and complies with all local housing and safety codes. Landlord is responsible for structural, plumbing, heating, and electrical maintenance at Landlord's sole expense.

5. TERMINATION AND NOTICE
Either party may terminate the lease at expiration by providing sixty (60) days' written notice. If Tenant must relocate for military or documented employment hardship, early termination is permitted upon sixty (60) days' notice with a one-month re-letting fee.

6. QUIET ENJOYMENT AND RULES
Tenant is entitled to peaceful and quiet enjoyment of the premises. Reasonable building quiet hours are observed between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM.
